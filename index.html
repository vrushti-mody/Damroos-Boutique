01 / OUR LEGACYQUALITY YOU CAN FEEL

A boutique built for real life.

Started in 1988, Damroos Boutique has 2 retail stores in Sion, opposite the famous Gurukripa Hotel. The first store focuses on sober, everyday wear shirts perfect for office and corporate setups, with 100% cotton, zero-maintenance fabrics that we manufacture ourselves.

Our Dios Damroos Studio, located on the first floor, is a 750 sq ft showroom featuring the finest and most exclusive fabrics — cottons, satins, linens, and our signature linen expertise. We also carry trousers, T-shirts, denims, sweatpants, shorts, kurtas, Pathani suits, and an ever-growing range of ethnic wear. Visit us for a clothing experience like never before, and enjoy our house-made chai.
